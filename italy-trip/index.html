Family-of-three baseline

Volotea load

  • Two paid 20kg checked bags.
  • Stroller + travel car seat as two free baby-equipment items.
  • Under-seat diaper/Hudson bag assigned to the lap-infant adult.
  • Priority for at least one parent if an overhead delay kit is needed.
Always accessible

Hudson transit kit

  • Formula/food and diapers for the travel day plus 24–48 hours.
  • Changes of clothes, medicine, thermometer, documents, comfort item.
  • Soft carrier for gates, baggage claim, stairs, and stroller failure.
  • Never put every formula container or critical item in one checked bag.

How this stays trustworthy

Repository first, website second

The private trip repository retains the original itinerary document and booking messages, separates confirmed facts from proposals, and records accepted decisions. This page is the shareable explanation of that record—not a parallel source of truth.

Sensitive Airbnb invitation tokens, reservation codes, passport details, and exact private addresses are deliberately excluded from this public page.
